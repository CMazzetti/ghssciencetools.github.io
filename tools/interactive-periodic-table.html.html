About the model: Bohr diagrams show electrons in shells around the nucleus and are the model used in NSW Stages 4–5. Neutron numbers shown are for the most common (or most stable) isotope, calculated from the rounded atomic mass. Masses in brackets are the mass number of the most stable isotope of a radioactive element. Shell configurations for elements beyond calcium follow the experimentally determined ground-state configurations (including exceptions such as Cu and Cr).
